Biography

I have been a Ph.D. student at the College of Integrated Circuits and Micro-Nano Electronics, Fudan University since 2025. I hold an M.E. degree in Electronics and Information Engineering at the College of Integrated Circuits and Micro-Nano Electronics, Fudan University in 2025. I obtained a B.E. degree in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at the School of Microelectronics, Fudan University in 2022.
